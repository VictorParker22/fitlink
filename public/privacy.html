Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

TLDR

FitLink collects only the data needed to connect coaches with athletes and run the coaching between them. We never sell your data, and nothing is used for advertising or tracking. You can delete your account at any time.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, date of birth (to confirm you are 16 or older) and, if you add one, a profile photo. A coach may also add a bio and a specialisation, which appear on their public page.
  • Training Data: The workouts, seasons and notes your coach writes for you; the sets, sessions and body weight you log; your weekly check-ins.
  • Photos & Videos: A profile photo, and any photo or video you choose to send in a chat with your coach.
  • Payment Information: Pass payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store card numbers; we keep a record of which pass you bought, when, and its status. A coach who connects payouts gives their identity and bank details directly to Stripe. Coach Elite is bought through Apple; we receive the subscription's status from Apple and RevenueCat, not your payment details.
  • Communications: Messages exchanged between coaches and athletes within the app.
  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, and a push notification token, used only to deliver the notifications you have allowed.
  • Website: A coach's public page on fitlink.coach can be read by anyone with its link. If you sign in or buy a pass there, your sign-in session is kept in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising cookies.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the FitLink service
  • To connect athletes with their coaches, and show each the other's training data and messages
  • To show your training progress
  • To process payments, pay coaches and manage subscriptions
  • To send push notifications about workouts, messages and check-ins, when you allow them
  • To draft a coach's reply with an AI assistant, only after the coach has agreed to it in the app (see section 4)
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse
  • To improve our app and user experience

3. Fitness & Health Data

The training data FitLink holds is what you or your coach type into FitLink:

  • The current FitLink app does not connect to Apple Health or Google Health Connect, and does not read data from any health app or wearable
  • Logged sets, sessions, body weight and check-ins are used solely to run your coaching: they are shown to you and to the coach you train with
  • Never sold to third parties
  • Never used for advertising or marketing purposes
  • If a future version connects to a health platform, it will ask your permission first, and this policy will be updated before it does

4. Data Sharing

We share your data only in these limited circumstances:

  • With your coach: Training data, logged sets, body weight, check-ins, photos you send and messages are shared with the coach you train with. A coach you leave keeps their own record of the coaching they delivered.
  • On a coach's public page: A coach's name, photo, bio and passes are public on the link the coach shares.
  • Payment processing: Payment and payout data is handled by Stripe. The status of a Coach Elite subscription comes from Apple and RevenueCat.
  • The coach's assistant: When a coach asks the assistant to draft a reply, the athlete's check-in text is sent to Google (Gemini) to produce that draft and for nothing else. The coach must agree to the assistant in the app before it is used, and reads and approves every message before it is sent.
  • Infrastructure: Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Push notifications: Device tokens are shared with the Apple Push Notification service (APNs) and Expo's push service for notification delivery.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law or to protect our legal rights.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Data is stored securely on Supabase cloud infrastructure
  • All data transmission is encrypted using TLS/SSL
  • Sign-in tokens are stored in secure device storage (the iOS keychain)
  • Each coach and athlete can read only their own rows and those of the people they train with, enforced in the database
  • We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information from your profile in the app
  • Deletion: Delete your account and its data at any time from Profile in the app, or follow these steps
  • Portability: Export your data from Profile in the app, or request it in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent: A coach can stop using the assistant at any time; notifications can be turned off in your device settings

7. Children's Privacy

FitLink is for people aged 16 and over. We ask for a date of birth at sign-up and do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16; if we learn that we have, we delete the account. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email.
