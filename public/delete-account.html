Delete your FitLink account

Applies to the FitLink app (com.fitlink.app) for iOS and Android.

In the app

Settings is the fastest route, and it deletes everything immediately.

  1. Open FitLink and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account, your data and your uploaded files are removed as part of that action. You are signed out when it completes.

No longer have the app

Email support@getfitlink.com from the address on your account, with the subject Delete my FitLink account.

We verify that the request comes from the account holder before deleting anything — that check protects you, so we will not skip it. Requests are completed within 30 days, and usually far sooner.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Deletion is immediate and permanent. It cannot be undone, and we cannot restore an account afterwards.

If you are an athlete with a coach

Deleting your account removes your data from your coach's view. Your coach keeps their own records of sessions they delivered, in the same way any business keeps its own books.

If you are a coach

Deleting your account removes your profile, your passes and your programmes. Your athletes' accounts are not deleted — they belong to those athletes, not to you. They keep their own data and can connect with another coach.

If you have a Stripe payout account connected, close it separately in Stripe. We cannot close it for you, because it is your account with them, not with us.

Cancelling a subscription is not the same as deleting

If you subscribed through the App Store or Google Play, deleting your FitLink account does not cancel that subscription — only Apple or Google can. Cancel it in your device's subscription settings first, or you may continue to be billed for an account that no longer exists.
